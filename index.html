A letter

A letter to my Bubu Baby

Take your time with this one.

“

My Chinni, my Bubu Baby, my Bangara,

Five and a half years ago, we began a story without knowing all the beautiful chapters that were waiting for us.

And somewhere along the way, you became so much more than the girl I fell in love with. You became the person I naturally imagine beside me when I think about tomorrow.

When I look back at our journey, I don’t just see the years that have passed. I see all the little moments that have become ours, all the things we’ve learned, and all the dreams we’ve slowly started turning into reality.

And now, we’re standing at the beginning of another chapter.

I know we still have things to figure out. We have plans to make, decisions to discuss, and a future to build. We might not have every answer today, but we don’t need to have everything figured out to keep moving forward together.

I want you to have room for your dreams, your happiness, your career, and everything that makes you who you are.

I want us to build a life where we listen to each other, respect each other’s feelings, and remember that we’re on the same team.

And Bubu Baby, even when we become husband and wife, I hope we never lose the little things that make us us.

I still want to make you smile with silly messages. I still want to take you on little dates. I still want to surprise you with flowers and gifts for no particular reason.

I want us to keep laughing together, sharing our smallest thoughts, and making ordinary days feel special.

I don’t want to stop dating you just because we get married.

I want to keep falling in love with you through every version of our lives.

Thank you for these beautiful 5.5 years, my Bangara.

Thank you for being a part of my story, and for letting me be a part of yours.

I cannot promise that every day of our future will be perfect. But I hope we always make space for each other’s happiness, communicate when things are difficult, and keep choosing to understand one another.

Today, I celebrate the story we’ve written.

Tomorrow, I look forward to the next chapter.

And for all the chapters after that, I hope we continue building a life that feels like ours.

You will always be my Chinni.

You will always be my Bubu Baby.

And you will always be my Bangara.

With all my love,

Yours, through every new chapter of our story.
